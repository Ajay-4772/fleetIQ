## 4. Jev / External AI Service Policy
- **Provider**: TypeSafe / Jev AI
- **Data Policy**:
  - VEHYRON only transmits synthetic, anonymized event payloads to Jev AI.
  - Never transmit proprietary credentials, real-world VINs, driver identities, or proprietary corporate data.
  - VEHYRON includes full deterministic rule fallback (`decisionSource = "RULE_ENGINE_FALLBACK"`), ensuring zero dependency on active external AI subscriptions for test suites and continuous integration.
